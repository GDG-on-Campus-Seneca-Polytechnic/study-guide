**You need:** a personal GitHub account and your Seneca email or proof of enrollment. **Cost:** free. No card needed. **Time:** 10 minutes, plus verification.

<Steps>
<Step n={1} guide="github-student-pack" title="Sign in to GitHub" alt="The GitHub sign-in page">

Sign in to your personal account at [github.com](https://github.com), or create one. Use the account you will keep after you graduate.

</Step>
<Step n={2} guide="github-student-pack" title="Add and verify your Seneca email" alt="GitHub Settings, Emails, with a Seneca address added">

Open **Settings**, then **Emails**, and add your `@myseneca.ca` address. Click the link in the email GitHub sends you. A verified school email makes approval faster.

</Step>
<Step n={3} guide="github-student-pack" title="Start the application" alt="The GitHub Education benefits page with Start an application highlighted">

Go to [github.com/settings/education/benefits](https://github.com/settings/education/benefits). Under **GitHub Education**, click **Start an application**.

</Step>
<Step n={4} guide="github-student-pack" title="Fill in the form and add proof" alt="The application form with the school selected and the proof upload area">

Pick Seneca Polytechnic as your school. If asked for proof, upload a document with a current enrollment date: your student card, a class schedule, a transcript or an enrollment letter.

</Step>
<Step n={5} guide="github-student-pack" title="Submit" alt="The Submit application button">

Click **Submit application**. You get an email when it is reviewed.

</Step>
<Step n={6} guide="github-student-pack" title="Turn on Copilot Student" alt="The benefits page with the Learn more link under free developer resources">

After approval, go back to the benefits page. Under the free developer resources section, click **Learn more** and follow the prompts to turn on Copilot. Copilot can take several days to switch on after approval. If you only see paid Copilot plans, do not buy anything. Wait and try again.

</Step>
<Step n={7} guide="github-student-pack" title="Claim partner offers" alt="The Student Developer Pack offers page">

Browse offers at [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack). Claim only what you will use; some offers start a timer when you claim them.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- **GitHub Copilot Student**: code completions in your editor, plus chat and agent use with a monthly allowance.
- **GitHub Pro** while you are a student.
- **Codespaces**: cloud dev environments, with a monthly allowance of core hours.
- **Partner offers**: about 80, for example cloud credits, domain names and learning platforms. Some need you to be 18 or older.

## If you are not approved

- <Check>students report that GitHub asks for billing information with your full legal name, matching your proof. No payment method is needed. This comes from community forum posts, not GitHub's docs.</Check>
- <Check>some students report that two-factor authentication must be on. Turn it on anyway; it protects your account.</Check>
- Make sure your proof shows a **current** date and your full name, then apply again.
- GitHub checks your student status again from time to time.
