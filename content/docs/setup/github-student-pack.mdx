**You need:** a personal GitHub account and proof that you are a current Seneca student. **Cost:** free. No card needed. **Time:** 5 to 10 minutes, plus review.

**Shortcut:** if you are signed in to GitHub, open [github.com/settings/education/benefits](https://github.com/settings/education/benefits) and skip to step 3.

## Before you start

- **A GitHub account.** Create one at [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup). Use an account you will keep after you graduate.
- **Your `@myseneca.ca` email, added and verified.** Add it at [github.com/settings/emails](https://github.com/settings/emails) and click the link GitHub emails you. Without it, the **Select this school** button in step 4 does not appear.
- **A photo of your proof (PNG or JPG).** A dated student card, a current transcript or an enrollment letter. It must show your full name, the school name and a current date. PDF and Word files are rejected, so screenshot a PDF first.
- **A browser that can share your location.** GitHub checks that you are near your school.

<Steps>
<Step n={1} guide="github-student-pack" title="Open Settings" alt="The GitHub profile menu with Settings highlighted">

Sign in to GitHub. Click your **profile picture** in the top-right corner, then click **Settings**.

</Step>
<Step n={2} guide="github-student-pack" title="Open Billing and licensing" alt="The Settings sidebar with Billing and licensing highlighted">

In the left sidebar, under **Access**, click **Billing and licensing** to expand it. GitHub keeps the Education page here. Applying is free and needs no payment details.

</Step>
<Step n={3} guide="github-student-pack" title="Start an application" alt="The Education benefits page with Start an application highlighted">

Click **Education benefits** at the bottom of that list. On the right, click the green **Start an application** button.

</Step>
<Step n={4} guide="github-student-pack" title="Fill in the application" alt="The application form with each field numbered">

1. Select **Student**.
2. Click **Select this school**.
3. Check that the school name says **Seneca Polytechnic**.
4. Pick your `@myseneca.ca` email.
5. Allow location. The button must say **Location shared**.
6. Click **Continue**.

</Step>
<Step n={5} guide="github-student-pack" title="Allow location if it is blocked" alt="Browser site settings with the Location toggle highlighted">

If the button does not say **Location shared**, turn location on for github.com, reload the page and try again.

- **Chrome:** click the icon at the left of the address bar and turn **Location** on.
- **Safari:** go to **Safari > Settings > Websites > Location** and set github.com to **Allow**.

</Step>
<Step n={6} guide="github-student-pack" title="Upload your proof and submit" alt="The upload step with the photo area and Submit Application highlighted">

Take or upload a **photo** of your proof. Make sure your name, "Seneca" and a current date are readable. Click **Submit Application**.

</Step>
<Step n={7} guide="github-student-pack" title="Check that it was submitted" alt="The confirmation message after submitting">

You see **Your application has been submitted**.

</Step>
<Step n={8} guide="github-student-pack" title="Wait while it is pending" alt="The application status showing Pending">

The Education benefits page shows your application as **Pending**. Review takes from a few minutes to a few days. GitHub emails you the result.

</Step>
<Step n={9} guide="github-student-pack" title="Wait 72 hours after approval, then claim" alt="The application status showing Approved, with the 72-hour wait highlighted">

When the status says **Approved**, your benefits unlock **72 hours later**. Copilot will not work before then.

After the wait, go back to the [benefits page](https://github.com/settings/education/benefits). Under the free developer resources section, click **Learn more** and follow the prompts to turn on Copilot. If you only see paid Copilot plans, do not buy anything; wait and try again. Then browse the partner offers at [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack). Claim only what you will use, because some offers start a timer when you claim them.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- **GitHub Copilot Student**: code completions in your editor, plus chat and agent use with a monthly allowance.
- **GitHub Pro** while you are a student.
- **Codespaces**: cloud dev environments, with a monthly allowance of core hours.
- **Partner offers**: about 80, for example cloud credits, domain names and learning platforms. Some need you to be 18 or older.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Select this school** does not appear | Add and verify your `@myseneca.ca` email at [github.com/settings/emails](https://github.com/settings/emails), then start the application again. |
| The button does not say **Location shared** | Allow location for github.com in your browser (step 5), then reload. |
| The upload is rejected | Use a PNG or JPG photo, not a PDF or Word file. |
| The application is denied | Retake the photo so your full name, the school and a current date are readable, then apply again. |
| Approved, but Copilot is not active | Wait the full 72 hours after approval. |

- <Check>students report that GitHub asks for billing information with your full legal name, matching your proof. No payment method is needed. This comes from community forum posts, not GitHub's docs.</Check>
- <Check>some students report that two-factor authentication must be on. Turn it on anyway; it protects your account.</Check>
- GitHub checks your student status again from time to time.
