---
title: Google AI for students
description: Claim a free year of a paid Gemini plan with your student status.
---

**You need:** a personal `@gmail.com` account, age 18 or older, proof you are enrolled at Seneca. **Cost:** free for the first year. **A card is needed** at sign-up. **Time:** 10 minutes, plus verification.

<Callout type="warn" title="Read this first">
In Canada the current offer is **one free year of Google AI Plus**, not Google AI Pro. You must claim it by **December 31, 2026**. After the free year it renews at **$6.99 a month plus tax** unless you cancel. The free Google AI Pro year for Canadian students closed in December 2025. Students can also get **Google AI Pro at 74% off** ($6.79 a month for up to 4 years, then $26.99).
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step n={1} guide="google-ai-students" title="Open the Canadian student page" alt="The Gemini for students page with the Claim a student plan at no cost button circled">

Go to [gemini.google/ca/students](https://gemini.google/ca/students/?hl=en-CA) and click **Claim a student plan at no cost**.

</Step>
<Step n={2} guide="google-ai-students" title="Pick Google AI Plus" alt="The University student exclusive offers page with Get student offer under Google AI Plus circled">

You land on **University student exclusive offers**. First check the avatar at the top right: it must be the Google account you want the offer on. If you see prices in another currency, you are on an account set to another country, so switch accounts. Then click **Get student offer** under **Google AI Plus**.

</Step>
<Step n={3} guide="google-ai-students" title="Confirm your account" alt="The Verify student status dialog with Continue circled">

The dialog shows which account the offer will be linked to. If it is right, click **Continue**. Google sends you to SheerID, which checks that you are a student.

</Step>
<Step n={4} guide="google-ai-students" title="Pick your country and school" alt="The SheerID form with the Country field circled">

Choose **Canada**, then search for **Seneca Polytechnic**. Type your first and last name exactly as they appear on your school records.

</Step>
<Step n={5} guide="google-ai-students" title="Finish the form and verify" alt="The bottom of the SheerID form with Verify student status circled">

Add your date of birth and email, then click **Verify student status**. If SheerID cannot confirm you right away, it asks you to upload a document that shows you are enrolled this term. <Check>which Seneca documents SheerID accepts.</Check>

</Step>
<Step n={6} guide="google-ai-students" title="Agree to the Google One terms" alt="The Google One Terms of Service dialog with Agree circled">

Back on the offers page, Google shows the **Google One Terms of Service**. Read them, then click **Agree**.

</Step>
<Step n={7} title="Add a payment method">

Google asks for a card even though the first year costs $0. Nothing is charged during the free year.

</Step>
<Step n={8} title="Set a reminder">

Put a reminder in your calendar a week before the year ends. Cancel in Google One before then if you do not want to pay $6.99 a month.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Verification usually takes minutes, but can take a few days at busy times. Attempts are limited, so do not resubmit again and again.
- Your verification is tied to the Google account you used. It cannot move to another account.
- You need to verify again each year to keep the student price.
- <Check>some Google pages for other countries list Canada as excluded. The Canadian pages say it is available. Confirm on the claim page.</Check>
