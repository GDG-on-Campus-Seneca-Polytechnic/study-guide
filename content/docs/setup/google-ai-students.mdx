---
title: Google AI for students
description: Claim a free year of a paid Gemini plan with your student status.
---

**You need:** a personal `@gmail.com` account, age 18 or older, proof you are enrolled at Seneca. **Cost:** free for the first year. **A card is needed** at sign-up. **Time:** 10 minutes, plus verification.

<Callout type="warn" title="Read this first">
In Canada the current offer is **one free year of Google AI Plus**, not Google AI Pro. You must claim it by **December 31, 2026**. After the free year it renews at **$6.99 a month plus tax** unless you cancel. The free Google AI Pro year for Canadian students closed in December 2025.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step n={1} guide="google-ai-students" title="Sign in to a personal Google account" alt="Google sign-in with a personal gmail.com account">

Use an `@gmail.com` account. School accounts, including your Seneca account, are not eligible.

</Step>
<Step n={2} guide="google-ai-students" title="Check the age on your account" alt="The Google Account age verification page">

Open [myaccount.google.com/age-verification](https://myaccount.google.com/age-verification). Your account must show you are 18 or older, and your birthday must match what you enter later.

</Step>
<Step n={3} guide="google-ai-students" title="Open the Canadian student page" alt="The gemini.google Canada students page with the claim button highlighted">

Go to [gemini.google/ca/students](https://gemini.google/ca/students/?hl=en-CA) and click **Claim a student plan at no cost**.

</Step>
<Step n={4} guide="google-ai-students" title="Pick the student offer" alt="The list of student offers">

Select the student offer shown for Canada. If the page lists a different plan or price than this guide, trust the page.

</Step>
<Step n={5} guide="google-ai-students" title="Verify you are a student" alt="The SheerID verification form">

Google uses SheerID to check you are a student. Upload a document that shows your full name (matching the form), Seneca's name or logo, and a current enrollment date. A class schedule or enrollment letter from Student Home works well. <Check>which Seneca documents SheerID accepts most often.</Check>

</Step>
<Step n={6} guide="google-ai-students" title="Finish the subscription" alt="The Google Play subscription screen with the payment method field">

Add a payment method and finish the sign-up. You are not charged during the free year.

</Step>
<Step n={7} guide="google-ai-students" title="Set a reminder" alt="A calendar reminder to cancel before the renewal date">

Put a reminder in your calendar a week before the year ends. Cancel then if you do not want to pay.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Verification usually takes minutes, but can take a few days at busy times. Attempts are limited, so do not resubmit again and again.
- Your verification is tied to the Google account you used. It cannot move to another account.
- You need to verify again each year to keep the student price.
- <Check>some Google pages for other countries list Canada as excluded. The Canadian pages say it is available. Confirm on the claim page.</Check>
