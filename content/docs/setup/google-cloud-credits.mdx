There are three ways to get Google Cloud credit as a student. Most people want the first.

| Option | What you get | Card needed? | Who can get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$300 free trial** | $300 to spend on Google Cloud for 90 days | Yes | New Google Cloud customers |
| **Google Skills lab credits** | 200 credits for guided labs, valid one year | No | Students, by request |
| **Course credits** | Up to $50 per student | <Check>not stated</Check> | Only through your instructor |

## The $300 free trial

<Steps>
<Step n={1} guide="google-cloud-credits" title="Open the free trial page" alt="The Google Cloud free trial sign-up page">

Go to [console.cloud.google.com/freetrial](https://console.cloud.google.com/freetrial) and sign in with your personal Google account.

</Step>
<Step n={2} guide="google-cloud-credits" title="Fill in your details" alt="The account information form on the free trial page">

Enter your country and account details. <Check>the docs do not name the buttons on this form.</Check>

</Step>
<Step n={3} guide="google-cloud-credits" title="Add a payment method" alt="The payment method form">

Add a card. Google may put a temporary hold of up to $1 on it to check it. You are **not billed** during the trial.

</Step>
<Step n={4} guide="google-cloud-credits" title="Wait for verification" alt="The Google Cloud console welcome page after the trial starts">

When the checks finish, your 90-day trial with $300 credit starts and the Google Cloud console opens.

</Step>
<Step n={5} guide="google-cloud-credits" title="Set a budget alert" alt="The Billing Budgets and alerts page">

In the console, open **Billing**, then **Budgets & alerts**, and create a small budget, for example $10, so you get an email if anything starts costing money. <Check>menu names in the Billing section.</Check>

</Step>
</Steps>

### What happens at the end

- The trial ends after 90 days or when the $300 is spent, whichever comes first.
- If you do nothing, your resources stop. After a 30-day grace period they are deleted. You are never charged unless you upgrade.
- To keep going, open the console's Welcome page and click **Activate**, then **Activate** again. Unused credit carries over but still expires at day 90.

### What the trial does not cover

Gemini API usage from AI Studio, GPUs, Marketplace products and quota increases are not covered. The free tier in AI Studio needs no billing at all.

## Google Skills lab credits

<Steps>
<Step n={6} guide="google-cloud-credits" title="Request lab credits" alt="The Google Cloud for students page with the Request credits button highlighted">

On [cloud.google.com/edu/students](https://cloud.google.com/edu/students), click **Request credits** and fill in the form. The credits pay for guided labs on Google Skills, not for your own projects. They expire one year after they are issued.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Course credits from an instructor

If a Seneca instructor applies for Google Cloud education credits for a course, you get an email link. Open it, confirm your school email, then click **Redeem now** in the coupon email and **Accept and continue**. The credit goes to a billing account named after your course and lasts one year.
