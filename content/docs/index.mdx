This is the study guide for GDG on Campus @ Seneca Polytechnic. It has three parts, plus the club's own tools.

## How a lesson is laid out

Every lesson has the same parts, so you always know where to look:

- **The header**: the track, the time it takes, and what you need, as chips.
- **What you'll build and the goal**: one card with the thing you will have running and what you will be able to do at the end.
- **How it fits together**: a diagram of the pieces and how they connect.
- **Before you start**: accounts and tools you need, with the details.
- **Steps**: numbered, one action each.
- **Concepts**: the ideas behind the steps, in a few lines each.
- **Troubleshooting**: common errors and the fix.
- **Official sources**: the pages the lesson was written from.

The lessons are short on purpose. They link to Google's official docs for the details, because those docs change faster than we can.
