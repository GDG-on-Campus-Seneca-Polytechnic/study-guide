- **When:** Friday, November 27, 2026, 1 to 4 PM
- **Where:** Seneca Helix
- **Bring:** a laptop and charger, and a personal Google account you can sign in to
- **Teams:** solo or in a small team

<Diagram name="jam-day" />

## Before you arrive

1. Make sure you can sign in to a **personal Google account**. School accounts may not work with AI Studio.
2. Get a free [Gemini API key](/docs/setup/ai-studio-key). It takes five minutes. You must be 18 or older.
3. Optional: try [your first Gemini call](/docs/ai/1-first-gemini-call), so your setup works before the clock starts.

## On the day

<Steps>
<Step n={1} title="Check in">

Check in at the door. You get a **voting code**. Keep it; you need it at the end.

</Step>
<Step n={2} title="Sign in with Google">

Sign in to your personal Google account in your browser, and open [AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com).

</Step>
<Step n={3} title="Get or check your AI Studio key">

If you do not have a key yet, follow [Get a Gemini API key](/docs/setup/ai-studio-key) now. Organizers can help.

</Step>
<Step n={4} title="Pick an idea">

Bring your own, or use **Jam Seed**: type the idea you have been sitting on, and it cuts it down to something you can finish in two hours with free Google tools, plus a prompt to start from. Jam Seed is coming soon; its code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/GDG-on-Campus-Seneca-Polytechnic/jam-seed). See [Club tools](/docs/club-tools#jam-seed).

</Step>
<Step n={5} title="Build for two hours">

Aim small: one screen that does one thing well beats five half-done features. Good starting points:

- AI Studio **Build mode**, which turns a description into a small web app.
- The [AI track](/docs/ai) lessons, for a script or a small server.
- [Firebase Hosting](/docs/web/1-firebase-hosting), to put it online so others can try it.

</Step>
<Step n={6} title="Show it and vote">

At the end, teams show what they built. Then vote at [gdg-code-jam-vote.tatandat110105.workers.dev](https://gdg-code-jam-vote.tatandat110105.workers.dev) with the code from check-in. The results appear on the projector. See [Code Jam voting](/docs/club-tools#code-jam-voting).

</Step>
</Steps>

## The apps on the day

The vote, the trivia game and Jam Seed are the club's own apps. Your phone talks to them after you scan a QR code, and the projector shows the results.

<Diagram name="club-system" />

If the organizers run a [Bean Bash](/docs/club-tools#bean-bash) trivia round, scan the QR code on the projector to play.

## Tips

- Keep your API key out of anything you share. If you show code on the big screen, check it first.
- If you hit the free tier limit (error `429`), wait a minute and try again.
- Stuck? Put your hand up. Organizers walk the room the whole time.
